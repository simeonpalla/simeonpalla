## About

I'm an M&E and policy analytics professional with five years of experience supporting government institutions and public-sector organisations across **energy, public service delivery, higher education and governance**. I work with large administrative datasets to measure programme performance, evaluate impact and turn evidence into decisions.

**Right now** I'm a Consultant (Power Sector Analytics) with the [Centre for Policy Research](https://cprindia.org), embedded in the analytics team of a state power distribution utility. I support results-based monitoring and evaluation of distribution-sector reforms, operational performance and energy-efficiency interventions.

## Featured project

### 🚦 ADB AI for Safer Roads Challenge 2026 — Speed Safety Score · **Top 10**
*Three-person team · Jun – Jul 2026*

Asked whether **posted speed limits are appropriate for the road**, rather than just measuring driver non-compliance. Used ADB-provided GPS speed data enriched with population, infrastructure, settlement and street-imagery layers across **Maharashtra and Thailand (14,711 road segments)**.

- Built a composite **Speed Safety Score** combining Safe System alignment, speed-limit credibility gap and vulnerable-road-user risk.
- Designed a **Priority Index** (Exposure × Likelihood × Severity) to rank roads for intervention under resource constraints, using population density, junction density and proximity to schools, hospitals, markets and transit.
- Grounded findings in the WHO Safe System approach, the Nilsson Power Model and AASHTO guidance.
